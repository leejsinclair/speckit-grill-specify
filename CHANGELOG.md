# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-11

- First release: grills each new spec straight after `specify` through a mandatory
  `after_specify` hook, with the grill setting (`on`, `strict`, `off`), a skip choice,
  stop and resume, the grilled-on label, a non-interactive exit and one outcome block.
- Carries upstream `speckit-grill-me` v1.0.1 verbatim, with one recorded change.
